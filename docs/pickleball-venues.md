# Pickleball 98: real-world venues (strategy, 2026-10-05)

The owner asked: "Is it possible to scrape the world map for all pickleball venues and basically build out those venues dynamically? ... play/walk around some of my favorite venues, play / drill with other people ... indoor or out ... an expansion of My Park."

**Short answer: yes, with OpenStreetMap as the only source.** OSM already traces most courts at the right size and angle, which is all a generator needs. The court-finder sites (Places2Play/USA Pickleball, Pickleheads) and Google forbid scraping or caching, so we don't touch them. **Phase 0 is built (section 0): the owner's seven favorite venues. Los Cab and SMASH are rebuilt against reference packs with walkable interiors (Fidelity workflow, below).** Sections 1-6 are the original research and plan.

**How it works** (files under `client/src/components/applets/pickleball/park/` unless noted):
- `tools/venues/fetch-osm.mjs`: one polite Overpass query per venue (25 s apart, 90 s back-off on 429), saved compact as `tools/venues/osm/<id>.json` (ODbL; committed so the build is reproducible). `venues.config.json` has each anchor and radius.
- `tools/venues/build-venues.mjs`: OSM + `tools/venues/overrides/<id>.json` -> `venues/<id>.json` (5-13 KB, lazy-loaded chunks of 2-4 KB gzip), `venues/index.js` (the picker's list + loaders) and `server/park/venues.json` (live-court counts and walkable bounds the server checks). It also runs the generator and saves `genExclude` (courts whose gates can't be reached) so the browser skips that check.
- `venuegen.js` (pure): spec -> layout spec + scenery: banks (courts side by side/back to back share a fenced box; pens that nearly touch merge; indoor courts stand alone with aisles), live courts (nearest the entrance, gate on an open side: a sideline at the end of a row, else a baseline; rack beside it; bleachers where they fit), every pickleball court numbered in reading order, the ball-machine court, pro-shop kiosk (indoors a front desk), benches, light poles, solid walls for buildings (one box per wall edge), halls (walls with a door cut by distance from the door point, an overhead lintel box), the bar's stools as seats with their own approach spots, a walking graph (5 m grid, 2 m indoors) and waypoints. Every live court is checked reachable on foot from the entrance; unreachable ones are swapped.
- `layout.js makeLayout(spec)`: Riverside is now just a spec (`RIVERSIDE`), with a parity test against the old constants (`riverside.fixture.json`, made by `tools/venues/snapshot-riverside.mjs`). Courts take any angle (`rot`); boxes are oriented (OBB) with a grid for big venues; routes use a heap and a spatial grid when the graph is big. The module's named exports are live bindings to the active layout (`setLayout`), so the rest of the park didn't change shape.
- `build.js` draws either Riverside or a venue; `scenery.js` draws a venue's own world (ground texture from areas/roads, every court via `courtkit.js` with per-court paint, fences/dividers/nets, buildings with gable or flat roofs, halls with one-sided walls, extras, trees by kind, cars in lots, backdrop); `courtvenue.js` is the engine venue for **your park game, on the court you walked up to** (the park's picture moved into the court's frame; indoors a cutaway so the match camera outside the walls sees in). This also fixes the old "played at the Riverside Park venue" leftover at Riverside itself.
- Online: `park:join { venue }`; each venue has its own parks (`server/park/index.js`), court counts and bounds from `venues.json`. Friends who pick the same venue meet.

**Override format** (`tools/venues/overrides/<id>.json`, dossier-friendly): `name`, `short`, `city`, `access`, `indoor`, `note`, `lit`, `crop` (m kept round the courts), `anchor` (a dossier's [lat, lon]: then points can be `{ en: [east, north] }` in meters from it; also `{ ll: [lat, lon] }` or `{ xz: [x, south] }`), `area` (keep only courts in a box). `courts`: `osm: false`, `sports`, `drop`, `dropNear`, `retag`, `add` (with `bearing` = compass bearing of the long axis, or `deg`; per-court `court`/`kitchen`/`surround`/`clay`, `pb` + `pbLayout` for lines on tennis), `grid`, `paint` (rules by place), `pbNear`, `pbOnTennis`. `colors` (court, kitchen, surround, tennis, tennisSurround, lines, pbLines, windscreen, fence, ground, far). `fence` (`join`, `room`, `dividers`: fence/low/net/none, `lightEvery`, `poleH`, `doubleHeads`, `netColor`, `netTop`, `dividerColor`). `buildings` (`drop`, `kind`/`color`/`roof`/`height` by id, `rules` by place with `roofStyle: "gable"`, `add` with `poly` or `rect`). `halls` (`building` id or `poly`/`rect`, `height`, `door`, colors `wall`/`pads`/`ceiling`/`floor`/`outside`/`roof`, `padH`, `beams`, `ducts`, `lightsStyle: "strip"`, `columns`, `bar`). `areas.add` (parking, lawn, paved ...), `trees` (`default`, `add`, `rows`), `extras` (tower, stands, cabana, gazebo, canopy, umbrellas, sail, terrace, mural, banner, living wall, spine, solar, golf, ocean, awnings), `backdrop` (hills, hillsHeight, mountains + mountainsDeg, skyline + skylineTint, palms, trees, treeKind, viaduct), `spawn`, `live`, `liveCourts`.

**Where you play (2026-10-07):** every venue here is also in Pickleball 98's **where & when** (Play, Practice, online rooms): a match plays on the venue's court picture (`courtvenue.js`) at your time of day (`timeofday.js`), on a court with a clear camera (`play/courtpick.js`). The index (`VENUE_LIST`) now carries `lat`, `lon` and `lit` for the sun and the night rule. Generator flags for a venue built only from sources: `fence.benches`, `fence.bleachers`, `fence.booth` false. Overpass fetches rotate to the mirrors (lz4./z.overpass-api.de, private.coffee) on 429/504. Details: `docs/pickleball-log.md` "One place".

**Adding a venue:** add `{ id, lat, lon, radius }` to `tools/venues/venues.config.json`; `node tools/venues/fetch-osm.mjs <id>`; write `overrides/<id>.json` (start with name/city/colors; check court counts in the build's output); `node tools/venues/build-venues.mjs`; `node --test client/src/components/applets/pickleball/park/venues.test.js` (add the venue to `IDS`/`EXPECT`); look at it in the browser (scratchpad scripts below); add it to the Help topic's list.

**Tests:** `node --test client/src/components/applets/pickleball/park/venues.test.js` (6: counts/indoor/angles/sizes, no overlapping courts, every live court reachable from the entrance with clear legs, interactables/seats/waypoints on open ground, walking graph connected, banks/walls/doors, any-angle frames, no NaN); `park.test.js` (22, incl. the Riverside parity test); `server/park/test/park.test.js` (11, incl. venues: own parks, courts, bounds). Browser (scratchpad `venues/`, vite 5205 + server 8005 with `VITE_SOCKET_URL=http://localhost:8005`): `e2e.mjs [ids] [--game] [--fps]` (picker, walk, stop, a court close up, watch, high angle, a park game on a non-first court and back), `online.mjs` (two phones at Whittier share a park and see each other walk; SMASH is separate), `perf.mjs` (CPU ms per frame), `game.mjs`, `map.mjs` (ASCII walkability map), `findnan.mjs`. Screenshots in `venues/shots/`.

**Measured** (390x844, Chrome headless on a loaded machine; the owner's own Chrome was busy): no throttle 49-60 fps at every venue (world 3.4-8.1 ms, draw 2.0-6.3 ms a frame; Riverside 5.8 / 3.8); 51-67 draw calls, 39k-86k triangles (Riverside 52 / 77k). At 4x CPU throttle 11-24 fps, but Riverside itself only reached 17-22 in the same runs (36 when it was first measured), so the 4x numbers are dominated by machine load; re-measure on the real iPhone.

**Left:** "Meet me at <venue>" from 98 Messenger; the regulars' rallies stand in for people's games on other screens (as at Riverside); the ball-machine court has no games; venue edits/suggestions (Phase 4) and the US-wide finder (Phase 1) are next if the owner wants them.

## Fidelity workflow (2026-10-06: Los Cab and California SMASH rebuilt; follow this for the other five)

The owner found the first pass "drastically off". The cause: it was never compared with images. Every venue is now built against a **reference pack** (scratchpad `venues/refs/<venue>/`, made by the reference fork, never committed: `aerial.jpg/.json` georeferenced Esri z20, `layout.json` traced courts/buildings/poles/pools/trees/parking, `colors.json` recommended albedo per surface, `floorplan.json` rooms, `details.json` props + arrival route, `photos/` + `photos.json` poses, `notes.md`) and checked with the compare tool until it matches. Reference images are for comparing only: commit numbers (colors, positions), never photos, logos or their text.

**1. Light and color are true.** Real venues draw through the Neutral tone curve with a calibrated rig (`build.js venueLight`: flat ground in the midday sun shows its spec hex, walls ~0.68, indoors bright neutral LED; per venue `light: { sky, sun, bounce }`). The old look lit venues with a sun stuck at its default position (the group's matrices are frozen before setDayLook moved it) through ACES: paint came out about twice too dark and hue-shifted (`compare.mjs truth`: flat cards mean dE 29 -> 6.3 outdoors, 6.2 indoors). Riverside keeps its own look. So: put the reference albedo in the spec and it appears on screen.

**2. Data layers** (`tools/venues/`):
- `overrides/<id>.refs.json`, generated by `node tools/venues/ingest-refs.mjs <id> --refs C:/Users/brand/98ish-reference/venues/refs` (the packs live there now, outside the temporary scratchpads): per-court paint by OSM id (`courts.byId`: court, kitchen, surround, alley), roof style by OSM id from the pack's roof classes (`buildings.style`: clay tile -> mansard tile band round a flat top with rooftop units, flat light -> flat with parapet and units), pools, tree canopies (small ones palms). Aerial colors are hazy: write `refs/<id>/palette.json` from the pack's `colors.json` recommended albedo (classes picked by aerial lightness: `pickleball { court, kitchen, kitchenPale, surround, paleL }`, `tennis { blue, slate, surround, slateL }`, `roofs { tile, flat, tileStyle, band }`, `pool`).
- `overrides/<id>.json`, by hand, wins over the refs layer (objects merge, lists join). Use the pack's aerial anchor as `anchor` so `en` points from `layout.json`/`floorplan.json`/`details.json` drop straight in. New fields: `groundStyle: "paved"`, `light`, `spawn { en, deg }` (an outdoor arrival works at indoor venues too), `courts.byId` (also `art: "bear" | "paddle" | "ball"`: our own center-court drawings), `buildings.style` / `rules` (`roofStyle` flat | gable | hip | mansard, `tile`, `parapet`, `hvac`, `band`, `windows: "mission" | "ribs" | "none"`), `buildings.toArea` (an OSM "building" that's really a lot), `buildings.doors` (buildings you can walk into), `areas.add` (`kind`: parking, deck, concrete, channel, lawn, turf, sand, pool with `lanes`; `color`), `rooms`, `props`, `fence.screen/screenH/dividerColor`, towers with `base` (rising from a roof).
- `node tools/venues/build-venues.mjs [id]` -> `client/.../park/venues/<id>.json` (+ index, `server/park/venues.json`).

**3. Room kit** (`park/propkit.js` pure, `park/props.js` draws; `venuegen.js`, `scenery.js`). A room: `{ id, type, name, poly|rect, h, floor, wall, ceiling, wainscot, partition, top, doors: [{ en, w, kind, gender }], furnish (false: only its own props), props, accent, sofa, chairs }`. Types with presets: lobby, proshop, cafe, bar, lounge, gym, studio, locker, restroom, sauna, steam, kids, office, spa, racquet, basketball, hall/corridor. Door kinds: glass, double, open, wood, restroom (our pictograms by gender), sauna, rollup, closed (drawn shut, no way through). A door cuts every wall it sits on (rooms, open buildings, halls), with a lintel overhead. Furniture keeps an aisle from every door to the middle of the room and never overlaps; stools and chairs become seats the regulars use. Rooms inside a hall or building draw their other side and a top only where they aren't the building's own wall. Each room is a zone: merged on its own and drawn only when you or the camera are in it or within 9 m of one of its doors (`scenery.cull`, called from world.js). The camera: the ceiling over you caps it, it's closer in a room and stays inside the room. Floors above the ground (2026-10-06): stairs (`stairs` in the spec) and decks are walkable; the walker carries a floor height `y`, `heightAt(x, z, y)` gives the floor under you at your level, boxes block only at their own height (`y0`/`h`), and `park/lift.js` raises a body's pose to its floor. Newport's rooftop terrace bar is reached by its stairs and walked across (rails round the stairwell: go round by the landing); SMASH's VIP mezzanine and Los Cab's ballroom are climbable too (venue polish, below).

**4. Prop kit** (`PROPS` in propkit.js, ~65 types): benches, picnic tables, chairs, stools, bistro and square tables, loungers, umbrellas, pop-up tents, cabanas, drinking fountains, bottle fillers, vending machines, bins, recycling, paddle wait boards, paddle racks, court signs, scoreboards, ball machines, bleachers, spas, treadmills, bikes, ellipticals, power racks, weight benches, dumbbell racks, mirrors, mats, lockers, sinks, stalls, showers, sauna benches, heaters, tile benches, desks, office desks, shelves, paddle walls, sofas, armchairs, coffee tables, TVs, rugs, plants, planters, bar counters, back bars, stairs, partitions, glass walls, kids' mats/toy boxes/tables, doors, lobby fountain, pool table, flagpole, basketball hoop, massage bed, starting block, ping-pong table. In a spec: `{ t, en, face (compass bearing its front looks to), c, w, h, s, y }` or `{ t, line: { from, to, every }, face }` for a row. A zone's props share one vertex-colored material and merge into one or two meshes.

**5. Compare until it matches** (`node tools/venues/compare.mjs <id> [truth|topdown|photo|color|solve] --refs <refs> --out <out> [--base http://localhost:PORT]`, vite dev on that port; `PLAYWRIGHT_CORE=<path to playwright-core/index.mjs>`; scratchpad `venues/fid/run.mjs` wraps it with the Chrome lock):
- `topdown`: an orthographic render with the aerial's exact box -> `topdown-side/overlay/edges.png` and every traced court's centre/angle error (Los Cab 51/51 within 0.12 m and 0.1 deg; SMASH 9/9 within 0.06 m).
- `color`: every court's service/kitchen/surround (alleys on tennis) sampled in the top-down render (indoors from under the ceiling) against its paint (= the pack's albedo): Los Cab 141 samples mean dE 6.9 (1 over 12, a surround sample on the apron), SMASH 27 samples mean dE 5.5.
- `photo`: renders every pose in `photos.json` beside the photo; poses solved by `solve` (`refs/<id>/points.json`: pixel <-> court feature `{ uv, courtAt: [e, n], c: [i, j] }` (i along the court, 0.3177 = a kitchen line, j across) or `{ uv, en, h }`) take precedence (`poses.json`). Los Cab's nadir drone frame solved to 2.1 px rms from 24 court features. Hold the field of view for straight-down shots (it trades off against height); 110-degree action-cam frames (SMASH's fly-through) are fisheye and don't fit a pinhole: use phone/club photos for those.
- `truth`: color cards under the venue's light (run after changing light or tone).
- Walk-throughs: scratchpad `venues/fid/walk.mjs <id> [--fps] [--only ids]` with `stops-<id>.json` (a scripted walk by hand from the arrival through the doors, checking you end up in each room, then a picture in every room and at outdoor stops, draw calls/triangles per picture). `reach.mjs <id>` checks every room is reachable on the walk graph from the arrival (Node).

## 2. Data sources and what their terms allow

| Source | Use it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenStreetMap via Overpass** | **Yes, the backbone** | ODbL. Court polygons, `surface`, `lit`, `covered`, `access`, fences (`barrier=fence`), buildings (`height`, `building:levels`), trees, paths, parking, enclosing parks and clubs. Public overpass-api.de: under 10,000 queries and 1 GB a day, and a "regular application" should stay around 1/100 of that (100 queries, 10 MB a day). Identify the app (User-Agent/Referer), no parallel queries, back off 30 s on 429, cache. Commercial use needs your own server (we're not commercial). |
| ODbL obligations | Manageable | A rendered 3D venue is a **Produced Work**: it needs attribution only ("© OpenStreetMap contributors" on screen). Our **venue index file and cached venue JSON are a Derivative Database**: publish them under ODbL with attribution (they're public static files anyway). In-app corrections merged into that data stay ODbL. |
| **Overture Maps Places** | Optional, for names | CDLA-Permissive-2.0 (Foursquare items Apache-2.0 with NOTICE). Good for naming the 98% of unnamed courts ("Lifetime ...", "... Rec Center"). Overture buildings are ODbL like OSM. It's GeoParquet on S3, so we'd build an offline extract, not query it at runtime. Joining it to OSM may make the result ODbL (fine). |
| **Places2Play (USA Pickleball) / Pickleheads** | **No** | Places2Play's terms forbid downloading, storing or publishing any of its data and prohibit scraping or crawling. Pickleheads powers that directory and its site refused our fetch (403); treat it the same. At most, link out ("Find on Places2Play"). |
| **Google Places / Maps** | **No** | Paid; terms forbid scraping and caching (place IDs may be kept, coordinates for at most 30 days, everything else must be fetched live with Google attribution). Breaks "free only" and can't feed a generator. |
| **Mapillary / KartaView** street photos | Not for v1 | CC BY-SA 4.0, but Mapillary's terms restrict building place databases from it. Possibly later as an optional "real photo of this venue" link, never as textures. |
| **Satellite imagery** | **No** | Esri World Imagery needs an ArcGIS license and allows tracing for OSM only; Mapbox needs a key and limits caching; Sentinel-2 is free but 10 m pixels (a court is about 1x2 pixels). Procedural looks instead. |
| **Elevation** (AWS Terrain Tiles / Mapzen Terrarium) | Later, optional | Free, keyless, attribution required. Courts are flat; only the backdrop would use it (Phase 3). |
| **Map UI tiles**: OpenFreeMap (vector, keyless, unlimited) + MapLibre GL | Yes, lazy-loaded | No key or quota. The OSM tile server's policy doesn't suit app use; avoid it. |
| **Place search**: Nominatim | Only "search on submit", proxied | 1 request a second for the whole app, **autocomplete forbidden**, identify the app. Venue search mostly runs against our own index, so this is only for "near a city". |

## 3. Recommended architecture

**Principle: precompute what's small, generate what's big on the device, cache everything, and send the server only tiny JSON.**

1. **Venue index (built offline, shipped as a static file).** A Node script (run monthly by a GitHub Action, free) runs one Overpass query per region, clusters courts into venues (60 m rule), names each from the enclosing park/club/building, Overture or a "Courts near <road>" fallback, and writes `public/venues/index-<region>.json`. One row per venue: `id` (OSM id of the anchor), lat/lon, courts, indoor/covered/lit flags, access, name, city. About 40 bytes a venue: roughly 400 KB raw / 150 KB gzip for the US, sharded by region and lazy-loaded. Served by Vercel, so it costs no Render bandwidth. Carries "© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL".
2. **Venue Finder (client).** Opens from My Park ("Choose a park...") or the title screen. Baseline: **Near me** (optional location prompt; the position stays on the phone and only sorts the local index), **Favorites**, **Search** (names in the index; "near a city" goes through the server's Nominatim proxy on submit). More options »: a map view (MapLibre + OpenFreeMap, lazy, on the Finder screen only) and filters (indoor, lit, 8+ courts, public only). Each row shows name, courts, indoor/outdoor, distance, a ★ toggle, and a "N people here now" chip.
3. **Venue detail fetch (server-cached).** `GET /api/venues/:id` on Render: if the venue isn't cached, the server runs one Overpass query (`around:220` of the anchor: pitches, fences, buildings, trees, paths, parking, water, sports-centre outline), reduces it to a **compact venue spec** (local metres, rounded to 5 cm; typically 5-15 KB) and stores it in MongoDB with caps: max 15 KB a spec, 2,000 specs (~25 MB of Atlas's 512), least-recently-used eviction, refresh after 90 days. Overpass is hit at most once per venue per 90 days, serially, with a 2 s gap; well inside the 100-queries/10 MB-a-day guidance. The client keeps specs in IndexedDB (`userKey`-free, device-wide, like map tiles: add to `GLOBAL_KEYS` storage notes) so a revisit is offline and instant. Render outbound: ~10 KB a venue, negligible against 5 GB.
4. **Generator (client, pure + Node-testable).** `park/venuegen.js`: spec → the same shape `layout.js` exports today (courts with frames, pens, gates, racks, bleachers, `BOXES`/`CIRCLES`, interactables, seats, spawn, bounds, a nav graph):
   - **Courts:** each polygon's minimum bounding rectangle gives centre, yaw and size; anything 12.5-14.5 x 5.8-6.8 m is a pickleball court; tennis courts tagged `tennis;pickleball` get 2 pickleball courts each, or 4 per court when it's a dedicated conversion.
   - **Pens and fences:** OSM `barrier=fence` lines if present; otherwise adjacent courts (a shared side within 1 m) form banks, each bank gets one pen with gates on the side nearest a path.
   - **Indoor:** courts inside a `building` or `sports_centre` polygon (or tagged `indoor=yes`/`covered=yes`) get a hall: walls from the footprint, a roof, high-bay lights, sports-floor colours, no sky. "Covered" means a canopy only.
   - **Surroundings within 120 m:** extruded buildings (height from tags, else 3.5 m a level, else one level), instanced trees (`natural=tree` points, scattered inside `wood`/`park` areas), parking asphalt with stripes, footpaths, water, low walls. Beyond 120 m: a skyline ring.
   - **Furniture placed by rules:** racks by gates, bleachers along the pen side facing a path, benches and lights along banks (lights only when `lit=yes` or indoor), plus a Locker Room booth and spawn at the entrance closest to parking.
   - **Colours:** `surface:colour` if tagged, else the classic blue court / green surround (editable later).
   - **Collision and navigation:** an occupancy grid (0.5 m) built from the boxes, with a nav graph from its open cells for regulars (you still walk only by hand, per the owner's rule).
   - **Budgets:** the scene uses `build.js`'s merging (static meshes merged per material, instanced posts/trees/rack paddles). Phone target: ≤ 60 static draw calls and ≤ 60k triangles for the venue; at big venues (44 courts) only the nearest 4-6 courts get live matches, the rest are "virtual" exactly like today, and far banks are drawn as merged boxes with painted lines.
5. **Refactor that makes it possible.** Today `layout.js` is hard-coded constants (4 courts east-west, fixed pens, a hand-written route). Step one is turning it into `makeLayout(spec)` with the current park as a built-in spec (`RIVERSIDE`), and giving court frames an arbitrary yaw (`toWorld/toLocal/yawToWorld/poseToWorld` currently assume a quarter turn). `world.js`, `build.js`, `regulars.js`, `followcam.js` and the server's `COURTS = 4` then read from the layout instead of constants. This also fixes the known leftover "your park game is played at the Riverside Park venue": the match renders on the court you actually walked onto.
6. **Multiplayer at a venue** (builds on `server/park/index.js` + rooms):
   - `park:join { venue }` puts you in the fullest instance under 16 *at that venue*. The server reads that venue's court count from its own cached spec, never from the client.
   - Racks and paddle stacking, `park:up` → solo or a private room, scores, lines/emotes and the bandwidth meter all work unchanged per court.
   - The Finder shows "N here now" per venue from a cheap server summary, refreshed when the Finder opens.
   - **Friends:** "Meet me at <venue>" from 98 Messenger (a link that opens the Finder on that venue) plus an opt-in "show friends where I'm playing" (off by default).
   - **Drills with a friend:** Practice's drills (`drills.js`, `practice/`) offered on any court through the same private-room handoff (one feeds, one drills).
7. **Favorites and edits:**
   - Favorites live in localStorage through `userKey` (synced like other prefs, if desired).
   - "This isn't right" edits (court count, indoor/outdoor, surface colour, name, missing lights) go into a small per-venue override record on the server: max 20 overrides a venue, 1 KB each, rate-limited, owner-approved or applied after 2 matching suggestions. Overrides are ODbL like the rest, and each one gets a "Fix it on OpenStreetMap too" link (osm.org/edit at that spot).
   - **"Add a missing venue":** drop a pin + court count + indoor/outdoor → the generator builds a generic venue there.
   - Because edits are stored per account, they need a Delete My Account eraser step and inventory line (CLAUDE.md rule), plus Help privacy text.
8. **Attribution and privacy.**
   - A "© OpenStreetMap contributors" line in the Finder, on the venue loading card and in Help, with the ODbL note for the index file; Overture/Mapzen notices if those get used.
   - Location is asked only when you tap Near me, used on the phone, never sent or stored.
   - No real logos or club branding in generated scenes: names appear as plain text only, and private clubs get a "members' club" badge.

  - Node tests (court detection on real samples, pens/banks, indoor detection, nav reachability, budgets) and a browser e2e (open Finder → venue → walk → play)
- **Phase 2, people (2-4 days):** per-venue instances on the server, court count from the spec, "N here now", Meet me from Messenger, drills with a friend, a server test for bytes per venue.
- **Phase 3, fidelity (3-5 days):** night lights and time of day (already in `sky.js`), weather from the Weather app (rain = indoor suggestion, wet look, wind flags), bleachers/shade structures from tags, terrain backdrop, regional court colours, ambient sound per venue type.
- **Phase 4, community (3-5 days):** suggestions/overrides with caps and moderation, "Add a missing venue", Delete My Account eraser, Help privacy topics.

## 5. Risks and mitigations

- **Coverage gaps / mis-tagging:** many tennis courts with pickleball lines are tagged `tennis` only, and indoor venues are under-tagged (10 `indoor=yes` in the US). Mitigations: the 2-per-tennis-court rule for `tennis;pickleball`; Add-a-venue; edit suggestions; nudges to fix OSM.
- **Unnamed courts (98%):** naming from enclosing areas, Overture, then a road-based fallback; users can suggest names.
- **Private clubs (about 10% in CA):** show them with a badge; a game visit isn't trespassing, but keep branding out.
- **Phone performance at huge venues:** the live-court cap, virtual courts, LOD crowd and budgets above; measure on the real iPhone in Phase 0 (My Park itself hasn't been phone-tested yet).
- **Overpass load or downtime:** server-side cache, a serial queue, 429 back-off, a fallback instance (private.coffee / VK Maps list "no limit"), and stale-while-revalidate for cached specs. The index is static, so search never depends on Overpass.
- **Render 5 GB / Atlas 512 MB:** specs ~10 KB, capped at ~25 MB in Atlas; positions as today (~18-31 KB/min per walking person) under the existing meter.
- **Legal:** OSM/ODbL only, attribution everywhere, no scraping of court-finder sites or Google.
