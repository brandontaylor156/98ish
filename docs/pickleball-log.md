**Pickleball 98 footwork + Locker Room** (2026-10-04, `applets/pickleball/`; not yet tried on a real phone):
- **Footwork** (`locomotion.js`, pure): a blend space (`blendSpace`: body-frame velocity -> weights for idle/walk/run/sprint/shuffle L/R/backpedal, cadence calibrated so stride x cadence = speed, clip `timeScale`s) and a step clock (left foot lands at cycle 0, right at 0.5; a foot lifts in its swing window, so a gait change can't skip a step). Planted feet are locked (`bx/bz`); the heel peels round the ball of the foot (`pivotOffset`, `contactPoint` for tests); swing paths sampled from the CC0 UAL walk/jog clips (`tools/gait-curves.mjs`). `facingFor`: face the net for shuffles/backpedals, `travel` (turn and run) for long fast moves (or sustained >2.6 m/s without a goal), `retreat` side-on for deep lobs, square up when a ball is coming; `turnToward` caps the turn rate. anim.js smooths velocity from positions (online), leans into acceleration, sinks on hard stops, swings arms against the legs (cos of the cycle), swivels the hips; athlete.js plays Walk/Jog/Sprint layers from the blend weights, locked to the step clock with each clip's contact offset.
- Measured (Node, pro doubles, 120 s, `pb3-gaitmetrics.mjs`): 4-5 m/s steps 11.8/s -> 4.7/s; pelvis snaps (>60 m/s^2) 3672 -> 316. Browser (`pb3-rally.mjs`): ball-of-foot drift while planted median 0 mm, p99 under 1 mm; anim cost about 21 us per player-frame.
- **Locker Room** (`LockerRoom.jsx`, data in `locker.js`): title screen button, Game > Locker Room..., Players > Locker Room...; tabs Body (Body A/B, 10 skin tones, 6 hair styles + colors, beard, height 0.94-1.06, build slim/regular/strong via `outfit.js reshapeBody`), Kit (themes Classic whites, Club colors, Pro kit, Beach, Winter, Retro, Casual with styles; tops tee/polo/tank/rash/jacket/crop/onepiece; bottoms shorts/skort/short/board/swim/pants; socks none/ankle/crew/knee), Gear (cap, cap backwards, visor, headband, bucket, beanie; shades/sport; wristbands; gloves; shoes), Paddle (6 designs). Drag to turn; viewer poses ready/jogging/standing (`engine.showcaseTurn/showcasePose`). Saved in prefs (`98ish.pickleball`: `looks` by character, `aiLooks` own/random, `autoVenue`). `lookForPlayer` dresses every roster in `Pickleball.jsx dress()`.
- New venues **Sandy Point** (beach) and **Frost Hollow** (winter, falling snow via `venue.update`); server allows them.

**Pickleball 98 upper body** (2026-10-04, after "legs fixed, upper body hilariously bad"; procedural, no new assets; not yet tried on a real phone):
- **Found** (filmstrips + Node/browser metrics): the stroke twist sign was inverted (a forehand finished with the paddle shoulder 33 cm *behind*: arm wrapped round the head); elbows flipped up to 145 deg a frame (hand targets cut through the shoulder; poles parallel to the arm); the forward swing aimed at `p.expect` but the AI hits the first moment `canHit` allows (median 18 cm off, often no forward swing at all); the hand roll flipped 180 deg round the handle (`gripSide`) and the forearm twist wrapped at +-180; the paddle face 15-21 cm from the ball at contact; claw-like other hand dangling; ready paddle in front of the face.
- **`strokes.js`** (pure): `chooseStroke` (dink/block/punch/drive/lob/slice/overhead/serve by kind AND the ball's real height), `chooseSide` (forehand/backhand by side; chest-high body balls at the net on the backhand; sticky), `strokeKeys` (back/contact/follow: hand, paddle axis, coil = shoulder turn, other hand, elbow pole, lean, crouch), `stepStroke` state machine (wind-up at a capped rate, shorter when late; forward swing is a Hermite that hits the contact hand exactly at tRel 0 and accelerates into it; follow-through for the shot actually played; hold; recover; unwind if the ball never comes), `predictContact` (the ball's physics + the match's reach rules, refined in quarter steps). Poses blend with `arcLerp` round the shoulder. Everything is authored for a standard posture (shoulders 1.3 m up, 0.1 m ahead) and moved with the real one (`ofs` in anim.js: crouch, waist bend, reach shift), so the contact point stays put.
- **`upper.js`** (pure): `ramp`/`smoothW` layer weights, `limitStep`/`limitTurn` pop limiters, `armIK` (fold limit 0.17 m, straight limit, elbow bend turned at a capped rate and kept when the pole is useless), `pushOut` capsules (torso, thighs), `lookToward` (neck yaw/pitch limits, top speed).
- **anim.js** layers: ready (paddle up in front of the chest, other hand at the throat) / relaxed between points; run arm pump off on the paddle arm while it holds ready (`W.pumpP`), 55% on long turned runs; serve hold; stroke; moods (fist pump with the elbow down). Hips lead shoulders (`hipTwist` spring). Out-of-reach contacts point the paddle at the ball. Situation adds `prep.id`, `prep.volley` and the predicted contact. Pose adds `bendP`/`bendO` (smooth elbow directions) and `info.stroke/style/fast/ready/offGrip/fist/aim`.
- **athlete.js**: arm IK poles = the pose's smooth bends; `steadyGrip` (hold the two-faced paddle whichever way is nearer last frame), forearm twist unwrapped (`unwrapNear`) and clamped; pop limiter (`limitQuat`) on spine/neck/head/clavicles; smoothed shrug; clip bone mask (run clips off the paddle-side clavicle while ready); other hand relaxed/cupped/fist (`setHand`); `probeUpper()` for tests.
- **Measured:** Node (pro doubles 3x120 s, `pb4-metrics.mjs`): face at contact median 15 -> 3 cm (p90 30 -> 11), predicted vs real contact 18 -> 0 cm, upper-arm max turn outside swings 145 -> 40 deg/frame, forearm 144 -> 39, head bob unchanged (4.3 cm).
