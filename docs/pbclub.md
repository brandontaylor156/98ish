## What it does (four tabs)
- **Play**: sessions ("Who's in?") and the seven courts. A session: venue (one of the seven or your own name/address), start, length, max players (2-32), courts (1-8), level range, note, invitations (Buddy List), "invitees can invite". RSVP in / maybe / out; past `max`, "in"s wait on a **waitlist** ordered by when they said in (a spot opening pushes "You're in!"). Running late (5/10/15/30 min, I'm here; the host gets a push). Chat (100 lines). Add to Calendar (`calendar/store.saveEvent` into the default calendar, reminder 60 min). Weather for outdoor courts within a week (Open-Meteo through `weather/weatherCore`). **Courts**: from 30 min before the start (or under More options), anyone who's in runs the rotation (`rotation.js`): Mix it up, King of the court (tap winners), Two groups (mixed doubles); the rotation is stored on the session so every phone shows the same courts; "Score this game" hands the four players to the scorekeeper. Courts list: Directions (Apple Maps on Apple devices, Google Maps elsewhere), Plan, **Meet Me Here** (an IM through `aim.sendIm` with the address and an Apple Maps link).
- **Score**: the courtside scorekeeper (`scoring.js` + `Scorekeeper.jsx` + `CourtDiagram.jsx`). Side-out (0-0-2 start, second server, the right-hand court serves first after a side out, server switches courts on each point) or rally scoring (server by score parity; optional "win only on your serve" freeze); to 11/15/21, win by 2/1, best of 1/3/5, who serves first; switch ends at 6/8/11; game point; Undo (400-state stack); speechSynthesis call (on-device); screen Wake Lock while a game runs; the game in progress is saved in `98ish.pbclub.live` (survives reloads/locks); Save logs the match (queued in `98ish.pbclub.outbox` while offline/signed off, sent on sign-on, `online` events and every minute).
- **Matches**: waiting for you (Confirm / That's Wrong; Agree to Remove / Keep It), queued on this phone, Log a Match (manual: up to 5 games), history (W/L, score, venue, status).
- **Ladder**: friends-only ratings (`clubCore.computeRatings`) for doubles and singles over everyone you've played or planned with, season filter (all time / this year / 90 / 30 days, sent as `season` to `/state`), your record, streak, best run, last 10, partners (W-L, point diff), head-to-head, venues.

## Rules (`client/src/components/applets/pbclub/clubCore.js`, shared with the server)
- Match statuses: `pending` (an opponent on 98 Messenger must confirm; the logger's partner can't; if the logger didn't play, either side can), `confirmed`, `disputed`, `expired` (7 days unconfirmed; computed on read and by the hourly sweep), `removing` (a player asked to take a confirmed match back; still counts until someone on the other team agrees; `keep` cancels), `unrated` (any guest in it: in records, not ratings).
- Ratings: Elo from 1500, one update per game, K 40 for a player's first 10 games then 24, margin weight 0.75 (11-10) to 1.25 (11-0), doubles partner-adjusted (each player's expectation uses 0.75 × own + 0.25 × partner vs the opponents' average). `levelOf(r)` = 3.5 + (r - 1500)/200 clamped 2.0-6.5 (a rough guide; Help says it's not official).
- Rotations (`rotation.js`): seeded xorshift, sit-outs by fewest sat then most played, 300 shuffles minimizing repeat partners (×10, squared) and opponents (×2); king: winners up a court (court 1 winners stay), losers down, pairs split, sitting players replace the bottom court's least-sat; mixed: cost +1000 for same-group partners, groups toggled per player.

## Server (`server/pbclub/`)
- `index.js` (routes, notices, pushes, sweep, eraser), `store.js` (MongoDB `pbsessions`, `pbmatches`, or memory without MONGODB_URI; rev-checked writes). Mounted at `/api/pbclub` in `server.js`; `pbclub.start()` runs the hourly sweep.
- Routes (POST, Bearer = 98 Messenger session token): `/state`, `/match`, `/match/:id {act}`, `/session`, `/session/:id/rsvp|invite|say|rotation|cancel`. Every account in a match/invite is checked against the AIM store (exists, no block either way). Logging a game you didn't play needs a session that you and every account in the match belong to.
- Live: `pb:changed { kind, id, by, invited?, confirm? }` on the 98 Messenger socket (`PbClubBridge.jsx` refreshes `utils/pbclub.js` and adds Notification Center items for invitations and matches to confirm). Push category **`pickleball`** (server/push CATEGORIES, NotifySettings): invitations, a session moved or cancelled, waitlist promotion, running late (to the host), a match to confirm, disputed, removal asked. Deep links `?open=program&name=Pickleball%20Club%2098&pbsession=<id>` / `&pbmatch=<id>` (`targetFromParams`).
- Caps: 2,000 matches logged per account, 60 a day; 30 upcoming hosted sessions; 32 players, 40 invited, 100 chat lines (300 chars), rotation JSON 12 KB; sessions deleted 30 days after the start. ~0.5 KB per match, 1-6 KB per session: a 20-person group playing 3×/week for a year ≈ 5 MB of Atlas. Traffic is tiny (JSON on demand; no polling).
- Delete My Account: step `pickleball` (inventory line in `server/account/index.js`): hosted sessions deleted (members told), the user taken out of others' sessions (RSVP, invitation, chat, rotation ids → "g:Deleted player"); in matches they played they become a random `x…` id named "Deleted player" so others' records and ratings don't change; matches with no other account and unconfirmed ones they logged are deleted.

## Tie-ins
- Buddy Locator's Add a Place has "Or a pickleball court..." (the seven `VENUES`, radius 100 m indoor / 300 m outdoor).
- Venue ids match Pickleball 98's My Park venues (`park/venues/`), but the club keeps its own `VENUES` list (name, address, lat/lon, indoor, access) so the two don't depend on each other.
