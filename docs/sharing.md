# Sharing with the phone (Web Share, share target, clipboard)
Moved from CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-05.

  - **Sharing with the phone:**
    - Out: `utils/share.js` `shareOut(payload, "phone" | "apps")` (Web Share API; call it straight from the tap, no awaits first, or iOS refuses) with fallbacks: download the file, or copy the text (a 98 message via `ShareCenter`). Payload rules are pure in `utils/shareRules.js` (tested: `node --test client/src/utils/shareRules.test.js`). "Send To > My Phone / Other Apps..." is in My Computer, desktop files, Notepad, WordPad, Paint, Sound Recorder, Photos' Share menu, Camera (File menu + a Share button after a shot), Calendar event "Share..." (.ics), Speed Typist "Share Result...", Word Duel "Share..." (plus "Copy"). `MenuBar` now supports submenus (`items` on an item).
    - In: `manifest.webmanifest` `share_target` (POST multipart to `/share-target`) -> `public/sw.js` keeps it in the `share-inbox` cache (not `98ish-*`, so updates don't clear it) and redirects to `/?share-target=<id>`; `OS-specific/ShareCenter.jsx` (lazy, on the desktop) shows **Received Items** and saves via `utils/receive.js` (pictures -> JPEG in My Pictures, audio -> whole Music 98 songs in My Music (type `song`, no 30 s cut; `docs/together-music.md`), videos -> My Videos (type `movie`, as they are), PDFs -> Documents (type `pdf`), all through `utils/mediaFiles.js` (over 8 MB: kept on this device only, with a note; `docs/storage-sync.md`), other files -> My Documents, links -> desktop Internet Shortcut and/or IE, text -> a Notepad document). GET `/share-target?title&text&url` works too (vercel.json rewrite for no-SW visits). **Only Android Chrome (installed app) and ChromeOS can be share targets; iOS Safari can't** (WebKit bug 194593, open as of 2026). iPhone path: "Upload from Phone..." (desktop right-click, My Computer File menu + toolbar Upload) and Paste.
    - Clipboard: `utils/systemClipboard.js`. Copying a file in My Computer/desktop (`fsActions.setClipboard`) mirrors its words / URL / picture to the system clipboard; Paste with nothing copied in 98ish (and "Paste from Device Clipboard") reads the system clipboard (pictures, links, text); Ctrl+V / the phone's Paste use the paste event. Notepad/WordPad Cut/Copy fall back to the Clipboard API.
    - Browser tests: `share-*.mjs` against `vite build` + `vite preview` (the SW needs a production build).
    - Wave 2 (2026-10-08): `shareRules.incomingKind` gained `movie` and `pdf` (`destinationFor`: sound -> My Music, movie -> My Videos, pdf -> Documents); the manifest's `share_target` accepts `video/*`, `application/pdf`, .flac/.mp4/.mov/.webm/.pdf; the service worker keeps shared files untouched in `share-inbox` until Received Items saves them (a big video sits there twice briefly). Send To of a big device-only file uses the Blob read ahead by `warmItem` (`peekMediaBlob`), so the share still starts inside the tap.

## Clipboard history (wave 2 part 2, 2026-10-08)
Like Windows' Win+V: the last 25 things copied inside 98ish, to paste again.
- **Pieces:** `utils/clipCore.js` (pure rules: caps, dedupe, pins, Clear all, password-field test, `sanitize`, `shrinkToFit`), `utils/clipStore.js` (a factory over IndexedDB, one database per 98ish user: `98ish-clipboard`, `98ish-clipboard-<id>`; the whole list in one record), `utils/clipHistory.js` (the app's store, `recordText`/`recordImage`, `installClipHistory()` from `main.jsx`, `openClipHistory`, `eraseClipHistory`), the panel `shared/clipboard/ClipHost.jsx` (eager, in App.jsx) + `ClipHistory.jsx` (lazy) + `clipPaste.js`.
- **What's recorded:** `systemClipboard.copyText`/`copyImage` (Notepad, WordPad, My Computer's Copy, Snipping Tool's Copy, Send To's copy fallback...; pass `{ history: false }` for anything secret), Paint's Copy (`recordImage`), Character Map's Copy, and every `copy`/`cut` event in the page (Ctrl+C, the phone's own Copy in a field or reading area: the event's own data if a handler set it, else the field's or page's selection). The same words again move to the top. Never from `type=password`, `autocomplete` current-password/new-password/one-time-code, or anything inside `data-clip="off"` (Calendar's private subscription link has it; so does the panel itself).
- **Caps** (`CLIP_CAPS`): 25 items, 256 KB each (text as UTF-8, pictures by decoded size; bigger pictures are re-encoded smaller as JPEG first, `shrinkToFit`), 4 MB in all. The oldest unpinned items go first; a new item is refused only when everything else is pinned. Clear all keeps pinned items; turning the setting off (`clipboardHistory`, Keyboard Properties > While typing > Clipboard, or the panel's More) erases everything.
- **Opening it:** Ctrl+Shift+V (Command+Shift+V on a Mac; a window capture-phase keydown, so it wins over apps; not while locked), the 98ish keyboard's **Clipboard** title-bar button (`docs/keyboard.md`), or `openClipHistory({ target })`. On a computer it's a small 98 window by the field with the first item focused (arrows, Enter, Esc); on a phone a sheet above the keyboard. Its buttons never take focus (`mousedown` cancelled, `data-kb-keep`).
- **Pasting** (`clipPaste.js` `pasteClip`): words into a text field or rich text go in at the caret through the keyboard's `typing.js insert` (execCommand insertText, value-setter fallback); anything else gets a synthetic `paste` event with a `DataTransfer` (a File for pictures) and `fromClipHistory: true` (Paint prefers that picture over its own clipboard); if nothing takes it, it goes on the device's clipboard and the panel says "press Ctrl+V" / "tap and hold, then Paste".
- **Privacy:** per user (database name per user id, like `userKey`), never synced or uploaded; `onUserRemoved` deletes a removed profile's database; `forgetAccountOnDevice` (Delete My Account) erases it. Help: the `clipboard-history` topic and the privacy-device / privacy-delete / delete-account rows.
- **Tests:** `node --test client/src/utils/clipHistory.test.js` (caps, UTF-8 sizes, dedupe, pins, Clear all, 4 MB of pictures, password fields, sanitize, one database per user, erase, a removed user, `load()` after copies, no IndexedDB). Browser (scratchpad `snip/snip-e2e.mjs desktop|phone`): Ctrl+C twice in Notepad, then Ctrl+Shift+V lists both, newest first; a pin moves to the top; pasting types at the caret and closes the panel; Clear all keeps the pin; a password box's copy isn't kept. Phone: the keyboard's Clipboard button opens the sheet above the keyboard with the copied snip and words; tapping pastes and the keyboard stays up.

## Snipping Tool (wave 2 part 2, 2026-10-08)
`applets/snipping/` (program "Snipping Tool", Accessories; Run/DOS `snip`, `snippingtool`). Ctrl+Shift+S / Command+Shift+S from anywhere opens it straight into a rectangle snip (App.jsx). Windows keeps Win+Shift+S for itself, so 98ish can't use that one.
- **Flow:** New snip -> the tool minimizes itself (and is left out of the picture anyway: `filter` + `data-snip-hide`) -> optional delay with a "Snip in 3..." badge -> the 98ish screen is copied -> Rectangle: the frozen screen with a dimmed chooser (drag a box; a bar at the top switches Rectangle / Window / Full screen or cancels, Esc too); Window: tap or click a window (`elementFromPoint` under the overlay; the desktop = full screen); Full screen: straight to markup. Then the tool comes back with the snip.
- **Capture** (`capture.js`): modern-screenshot 4.6.8 (MIT, qq15725; DOM -> foreignObject SVG -> canvas) loaded once from jsDelivr at a pinned URL (`snipMath.js` `SHOT_URL`, the same pattern as PDF Viewer's pdf.js; offline before the first load, it says so). Scale `min(2, devicePixelRatio)`.
  - **WebGL canvases:** `utils/canvasKinds.js` (installed in `main.jsx`) remembers each canvas's context kind by wrapping `getContext`. Before the copy, `grabWebGL` wraps `window.requestAnimationFrame` for up to 12 frames / 1.5 s: after every callback it samples 16 pixels of each 3D canvas with `readPixels` and, on the first frame with anything drawn, calls `toDataURL` right there, before the browser clears the drawing buffer (canvases with `preserveDrawingBuffer` are read at once). Each 3D canvas's `toDataURL` is then overridden on the instance while modern-screenshot clones it. One that drew nothing in that time (paused, drawn on demand, or a game holding its own reference to rAF) becomes a gray striped "3D picture" box, never blank black.
  - Cross-origin iframes (YouTube, Compass pages) can't be read: their clones get a hatched background.
  - On computers, **Capture another window or screen...** (More options) takes one frame with `getDisplayMedia` (not offered on phones: iOS has no getDisplayMedia).
- **Markup:** steps are plain data (`{ type: "stroke", stroke }` in the picture's pixels at that time, `{ type: "crop", rect }`); `compose` redraws the original with every step in order, and `layout` (pure) gives the final size and offsets; the live stroke draws on a second canvas. Pen (red/blue/black/green, thin/thick under **Pen »**), Highlighter (yellow, multiply, 40%), Crop (drag a box; applied at once), Undo (Ctrl+Z). Widths are in screen px at any zoom (`strokeWidth`). A snip shows at most at its real size on screen.
- **Out:** the PNG (a JPEG over ~1.5 MB, `pickFormat`) is made ahead after every change, so **Send to My Phone** calls `shareOut` straight from the tap (iOS needs that). **Save** -> `photos/library.savePicture` in C:\My Pictures as "Snip 2026-10-08 at 14.32.05.png" (then Open -> Photos); **Copy** -> `copyImage` (also into the clipboard history, shrunk to 256 KB). Ctrl+S / Ctrl+C in the window.
- **Tests:** `node --test client/src/components/applets/snipping/snipMath.test.js` (drag boxes, clamping, CSS -> picture pixels at 2x/3x, fit, view -> picture points, stroke widths, point thinning, bounds, crop steps, layout through crops, names, formats, the pinned CDN URL). Browser (scratchpad `snip/snip-e2e.mjs`, `GL=1` for SwiftShader WebGL): desktop 1280x800 rectangle of Notepad (capture ~1.5 s) with pen, highlighter, crop + Undo, saved to My Pictures; Hover's window in Window mode (WebGL frame grabbed: ~380 colors, not black); a WebGL canvas that drew once and stopped shows the gray box; phone 390x844: New snip on screen, a rectangle at 2x, Save/Copy/Send bottom at 806 px, saved, copied.
- **Limits / not done:** not tried on a real iPhone (Safari draws foreignObject more slowly and has had image-loading quirks; modern-screenshot redraws for Safari). Videos and cross-origin images without CORS come out blank or as placeholders. A big DOM (many windows) takes a few seconds.
