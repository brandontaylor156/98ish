# Watch & Listen Together and Music 98 (2026-10-05)

## Watch & Listen Together
- **What:** a YouTube video (plus an Up Next queue) playing in sync for an IM conversation or a chat room, with floating reactions and a small chat strip. Program "Watch Together" (`applets/together/`, group Internet, also Entertainment and Us). Started from the IM header's **Watch** button (next to Call; icon-only on phones, like Call/Video Call now), a chat room's **Watch Together** button, the program's start screen (pick an online buddy or a joined room, optional link, YouTube search under More options), or YouTube '98's Share dialog (**Watch Together...**).
- **Server** (`server/aim/together.js`, bound in `server/aim/index.js` like `calls.js`): relays control state only, in memory, nothing saved (so no Delete My Account step; Help's privacy-server lists it under "Only in memory"). A session: `{ id, conv ("im:a|b" | "room:<key>"), host, members (IM: the two keys), room, joined: Map(key -> { since, lostTimer }), queue: [{ id, v (11-char video id), title (<= 100), by }], index, playing, pos, at (server ms), rate, anyone, chat (last 30), rev }`. Expected position = `pos + (now - at)/1000 * rate` while playing. Events (all acked): `tg:start { with | room, video?, title?, start? }` (an open session for the same conversation is joined instead, and the video queued), `tg:join`, `tg:leave`, `tg:end` (host), `tg:cmd { op: play|pause|seek|rate|select|next|prev|ended }` (`ended` counts once per index), `tg:queue { op: add|remove|move }`, `tg:settings { anyone }` (host), `tg:react` (8 emoji), `tg:say` (<= 200 chars), `tg:time` (clock sync), `tg:mine` (sessions you may join). Server -> client: `tg:invite`, `tg:state`, `tg:react`, `tg:say`, `tg:end`. Who may join: IM = the two people (neither blocking); room = whoever is in the room now (leaving the room leaves the session). Host leaves -> longest-present person; a dropped socket is marked away for 20 s (`LOST_MS`); empty sessions end after 60 s; idle 6 h. Blocking ends a two-person session; signing off leaves all. Invites: `tg:invite` to online members + a push (category `im`, DND holds it) with `url: /?open=program&name=Watch%20Together&together=<id>` (`targetFromParams` turns `together=` into a handoff).
- **Caps / budget:** 300 sessions server-wide, 3 hosted per person, 20 people, 100 queue items, rate limits (40 commands, 8 chat lines, 20 reactions per 10 s; 6 starts a minute). Traffic is a few hundred bytes per change, so Render's 5 GB/month isn't touched; the video streams from YouTube to each person.
- **Client:** `syncCore.js` (pure: `parseYouTube` (watch/youtu.be/shorts/embed/live/music + `t=`), `offsetFrom` (fastest round trip), `expectedPos`, `decide` (seek when > 0.6 s off with 0.25 s lead, 1.5 s cooldown, don't re-seek while buffering a little behind; play/pause follow), `userJumped` (someone scrubbed YouTube's own bar: shared as a seek)), `togetherStore.js` (module state outside React; AimContext forwards `tg:*` events and hands over `request` while signed on; a fresh sign-on re-joins), `player.js` (official YouTube IFrame Player API, `controls: 1`, `playsinline`; a clock-driven stub with the same shape when `localStorage["98ish.together.stub"] = "1"` or `window.__togetherStub`, exposed as `window.__togetherPlayer`), `Together.jsx` (start view; session view: player stage (16:9, min 200 px tall: YouTube's minimum), controls, reactions, chat, Add a video; More options: Up Next, Speed, Anyone can control (host), Mini player, End for Everyone). Titles: YouTube '98's `/api/youtube?action=video`, then YouTube oEmbed (CORS works), then "YouTube video". Changes made by YouTube's own controls are detected (state change not caused by us within 900 ms) and shared, or undone for people without control. iPhone: a "Tap to join the video" button appears when the video should play but hasn't started for 2 s (iOS wants a tap); YouTube stops in the background/locked screen (Help says so).
- **AimContext:** `tg:invite` -> a system line with `action: { kind: "together", id, label: "Join" }` in the IM (or room) transcript (`TranscriptLine` renders the button through `onAction`), IM window pops up unfocused unless DND, `notify()` with a program target. `aim.openTogether(handoff)` opens the window (`{ with, start: true }`, `{ room, start: true }`, `{ together }`, `{ video, title }`).
- **Made self-explanatory (2026-10-07, owner: "how the hell does this even work"):** the start screen is three numbered steps: **1 Video** (one box: a YouTube link, or words + **Search**, results inline; "No video yet? That's fine"), **2 Watch with** (Online now / Chat rooms / Not signed on (they get a notification) from the whole Buddy List, or "No buddies yet" with how to add one), **3 Start watching with <name>**, plus a "How does Watch Together work?" link to Help. Signed off: the three steps (`HowItWorks`) and **Sign on to 98 Messenger**. In a session: a yellow "Invitation sent to X. When they tap Join..." line until the other person joins (`state.with`), a clearer empty stage ("Nothing playing yet" + what to do, or "Waiting for <host> to pick a video"), and **▶ Play for everyone** on the stage when a video is cued. Messenger entry points: the IM's **Watch** button keeps its word on phones and now opens the start screen with that buddy picked (it used to start an empty session at once), Buddy List right-click/hold **Watch Together...** and People > **Watch Together...**. Help topic `watch-together` starts with "In short" and the steps. Browser walk-through (scratchpad `tg.mjs`, two contexts, stub player): register two accounts, add each other, Alice starts with a link -> waiting line; Bob's IM shows Join -> joins; Play for everyone -> both players 4.07/4.06 s; Bob pauses -> Alice pauses. No sync bugs found.
- **Not done:** syncing a Music 98 song (it would mean uploading people's music files to the server; skipped). No computer players; no "watch with someone offline" beyond the push invite.

## Music 98
- **What:** your own songs in a Windows Media Player 7-style window (`applets/music/`, program "Music 98", app `musiclib`, group Entertainment). Songs are drive files of a new type **`song`** (`FILE_TYPE.song`): the original MP3/M4A/AAC/WAV/FLAC/OGG bytes as a data URL, imported to C:\My Music (max 60 MB each). Opening a song file plays it (and its folder) in Music 98 (`openItem`); Send To/download gives the original file back (`fileTransfer.exportFile` case `song`). **Uploads from the phone and the share target now keep audio whole as songs** (`receive.js` `saveIncoming` -> `importSongs(files, null, dir)`), instead of the 30 s WAV cut; Sound Recorder recordings are unchanged.
- **Library** (`musicStore.js`): details per content hash in localStorage `98ish.music` (per user via the storage seam): title, artist, album, album artist, track, disc, year, genre, duration (measured with an Audio element at import), art key, plus playlists and prefs (shuffle, repeat, eq, viz, view). Songs that arrive without details (sync, copies) are read in the background (50 at a time). Album art: 256 px JPEGs in IndexedDB `98ish-music` (`-<user id>` for extra users, deleted by `onUserRemoved`), shared per picture. `tidy()` drops details of songs no longer in the drive. My Music isn't a default sync folder, so songs stay on the device unless the person adds it in Backup.
- **Tags** (`tags.js`, pure): ID3v2.2/2.3/2.4 (encodings, unsynchronisation, extended header, syncsafe sizes, APIC/PIC front cover), ID3v1 (+ genre numbers), MP4 `ilst` (`©nam ©ART ©alb aART ©day ©gen gnre trkn disk covr`), FLAC (VORBIS_COMMENT, PICTURE), Ogg Vorbis/Opus comments; `guessFromName("02 - Artist - Title.mp3")`; `mimeFor`.
- **Player** (`engine.js`, module singleton): two `<audio>` elements (the next song loads 20 s before the end, so it starts on `ended`), blob URLs from the drive contents, queue/shuffle/repeat logic from `library.js` (pure: grouping albums/artists, sorting ("The" ignored), search, `makeQueue`, `reshuffle` keeping the current song, `nextPos`/`prevPos` (3 s rule), `insertNext`, `append`, `dropKey`), Media Session metadata + actions + position state (lock screen / Control Center / media keys), `claimPlaybackSession` (iPhone ringer switch), volume = taskbar `masterGain()` on the element (iPhone ignores it: side buttons). Equalizer (low shelf 120 Hz, peaking 1 kHz, high shelf 7 kHz, +-12 dB) and visualizer route the elements through Web Audio (`createMediaElementSource` once per element; `shutDown` closes the context and makes fresh elements). Minimizing keeps playing; closing the window stops. Dev hook `window.__music`.
- **UI:** Now Playing panel (art, title, artist, visualizer), seek bar, round chrome transport (shuffle, prev, play, next, repeat), tabs Songs/Albums/Artists/Playlists/Queue, Search, **Add Songs...**; each row's **...** button (a ContextMenu, also right-click) has Play, Play Next, Add to Queue, Add to Playlist (new or existing), Remove from Playlist, Edit Info...; More options: Equalizer (3 sliders), Visualizer, notes. Phones: tabs on their own row, then Search + Add Songs....
