# Live Broadcast (Pickleball 98): a real game, watched live in 3D

Built 2026-10-06 (the next "groundbreaking" feature after Twin Replay / Twin Clones). Inside Pickleball 98 only (no program of its own): **Real Games > Go Live** (Play tab card) or **Game > Go Live (friends watch in 3D)...** → screen `live`; watching → screen `watch` (deep link `?open=program&name=Pickleball%2098&live=<12 hex>[&code=<10 hex>]`, the push's URL; Real Games' "<name> is live" buttons; My Park's LIVE strip). Help: the "Go Live" section of the `pickleball` topic; privacy line in `privacy-server` ("Only in memory").

## How it works
- **Filming phone** (`twin/live/GoLive.jsx` + `liveReader.js`): setup (venue/court from Twin Replay's `venues.js`, names: near side first, "tell my buddies") → camera (`getUserMedia` back camera 1280x720 ideal; mic without echo cancellation/noise suppression/AGC, camera alone if the mic is refused) → corners (`Calibrate` with `snap`: a live picture, "New picture") → `bc:start` → live. The reader is Twin Replay's pipeline run on the camera's current frame: 256 px crops round each tracked player, court tiles (all while nobody's found, else 3 at a time, every frame while someone's missing / every ~1.2 s), MediaPipe Pose Landmarker lite in the worker (GPU, CPU fallback), `createAnalyzer` tracker. Old landmarks are dropped from track samples after 2.5 s (memory over an hour). The mic is a ScriptProcessor ring at 12 kHz; every 0.3 s `detectOnsets` runs on the last 2.5 s. Wake Lock while live.
- **Hits live** (`liveHits.js`, pure): each pop goes to the other team's player whose wrist peaked within -0.28..+0.18 s once the swing after it has been seen (`settle` 0.25 s); a strong pop accepts a weak swing. No mic: swing peaks, decided 0.6 s late so the stronger of a wind-up/swing pair wins (as `findHits`). `createSlots`: tracks → broadcast slots (near team first, never reused).
- **Ticks** (`packet.js`): own 10 Hz timer, independent of the model's pace. 34 bytes for four players: u8 version, u8 n, u32 t ms, per player i16 x cm, i16 z cm, i8 vx, i8 vz (0.1 m/s), u8 seen. Between readings players are carried along their velocity (≤ 0.3 s). `seen` = found in the latest pictures read (not "captured < 1.5 s ago": on a slow phone every reading is older than that; that bug made viewers drop every tick). Events JSON: hit {t, p, h cm, s fh/bh, src}, score {a, b, call, over} (Point: near / far taps → Real Games' `scoring.js`), roster. Raw keypoints aren't sent (motion matching animates bodies from position/velocity/hits; 30x the bytes for nothing visible).
- **Server** (`server/broadcast/index.js`, wired in `server/net` as `liveCasts`, push via `server.js`): memory only. `bc:start/tick/ev/stop/watch/unwatch/react/list`. Tick shape checked (`validTick`), events cleaned (same rules as `packet.js cleanEvent`, kept in step by hand: CommonJS can't load the ESM file). Ring: last 60 s of ticks + hits; the late joiner gets them as ONE concatenated buffer (`splitTicks` on the client; ~600 separate attachments made `bc:watch` time out). Who may watch: the host, the host's buddies (not blocked either way), or anyone with the code. Caps: 12 broadcasts, 30 viewers each, 25 ticks/s and 10 events/s per host, 1 reaction/s, 3 h per broadcast; one watch per person. Buddies get `bc:live` (Notification Center via `PbClubBridge`) and a push (category `pickleball`, at most every 30 min per host and buddy). Nothing persists → no Delete My Account step.
- **Viewers** (`LiveWatch.jsx` + `stream.js`): `createStream` keeps ticks in order (late ones inserted), hits, score, roster; `analysis()` → Twin Replay's `buildRallies` (hits carry `height0`/`side0`) + `withPaths`; `createCursor` builds `buildFrames` for [built, newest - DELAY (1 s)] every 150 ms and the engine plays them (`playTwin({ live: true })` + `twinAppend`: a live replay waits at its newest frame instead of ending; keeps 75 s for rewind). Falling > 2.5 s behind (slow phone, tab slept) catches up unless the viewer rewound; ⟲ 10 s / Go live. Cameras Broadcast/Side/Top/Follow, score bug, reactions, viewer count, end reason, **Save to Twin Replay** (`toAnalysis()`).
- **My Park** (`useLiveCourt.js` + `world.setLive`): at a venue where a buddy is live (bc:list match on venue), the viewer watches it and the real players walk on that court (court frame → world via `toWorld`/`dirToWorld`, 20 updates/s from `sampleAt(edge)`), labelled "here for real · live · <host>'s game"; a LIVE strip with **Watch**. `setReal`'s cleanup now only removes `real:` bodies.
- **After the game**: the filming phone saves its own copy of what it sent (`createStream({ keep: Infinity })` fed with its ticks/events) as a Twin Replay game (`live: true`, no video); viewers can save theirs.

## Measured (2026-10-06, this machine: headless Chrome, CPU-only MediaPipe, loaded)
- **End to end** (`scratchpad/live/lv.mjs [seconds] [slow]`): A = 844x390 landscape phone whose camera is the engine-rendered fence-cam rally (Chrome fake capture: `rally.mjpeg` / mic `rally.wav`, made with ffmpeg from Twin Replay's `e2e.webm`), going live through the real UI; B = 390x844 touch phone opening the share link 8 s later. All checks pass: viewer playing ~26-35 s after opening the link (page load on a machine busy with the model), network latency median 4-10 ms (p90 24-191 ms) + the 1 s buffer, viewer positions identical to what was sent (1,140 compared, 0.000 m), names, 10 s back (10.4 s behind) and back to live (0.3 s), reaction reaches the filming phone, end seen by the viewer, both save to Twin Replay, no sideways scroll, no page errors.
- **Broadcaster speed here**: 0.16-0.29 pictures/s (3.1-5.7 s per picture on CPU WASM with crops + tiles). Ticks still go out 5-8/s (the model's worker competes for this machine's CPU; target 10/s).
- **Tracking accuracy**, emulating a ~20x faster device (`slow`: the rally slowed 20x as a 2 fps 944x598 y4m, silent mic, judged in game time after finding the loop phase): median 0.51 m, p90 1.52 m over 2,874 samples (Twin Replay offline on the same video: 0.38-0.52 m median). Hits weren't measurable live here (too few pictures per swing at this machine's pace; the unit test covers them).
- **Bandwidth**: 34 bytes a tick → ~340 B/s at 10 Hz in, the same out per viewer (+ socket.io framing). Measured payload 159-270 B/s at this machine's 5-8 ticks/s. Server test: an hour, one viewer, < 3 MB of payload. Render budget: a 2-hour broadcast with 3 viewers is 4 streams x 340 B/s x 7,200 s ≈ 10 MB of payload, ~30 MB with socket.io framing; 3 broadcasts a week (~12 a month) ≈ 0.4 GB, well inside the 5 GB/month.
- **My Park** (`scratchpad/live/lp.mjs`): host and friend sign on, the host adds the friend, the host goes live at Los Cab court 0; the friend walks into My Park at Los Cab: the LIVE strip shows, 4 live players on the court, they move, Watch opens the live view, no page errors.

## Tests
- `node --test client/src/components/applets/pickleball/twin/live/live.test.js` (5): packets (round trip within 1 cm, clamping, malformed refused, events cleaned, the joined late-join buffer split), live hits on the synthetic rally read frame by frame with pops heard ~0.1 s late (all 9 within 0.15 s, right team, decided < 0.5 s after the pop; swings only ≥ 7/9), slots, the stream through a jittered/reordering network (frames contiguous at 1/30 s, never ahead of the buffer, positions within 0.35 m of the tracked ones, hits heard, roster, savable), the cursor jumping after a sleep.
- `node --test server/broadcast/test/broadcast.test.js` (4, in root `npm test`): tick/event checks, buddies told + pushed (not the blocked), relay, late joiners get ~60 s, viewer can't send, reactions limited, end, second broadcast within 30 min not pushed again; who may watch (buddy / link / stranger / blocked), viewer and broadcast caps, leaving; tick rate limit and an hour's bandwidth.
- Browser: `lv.mjs` and `lp.mjs` above (Chrome lock).

## Limits / next
- Not tried on a real iPhone. The first real test: a phone on a real fence, sound on.
- A phone's GPU should read several pictures a second; this machine couldn't show live hit detection (only the unit test does).
- One live game per person at a time (watching or in the park); the park shows buddies' games only.
- The ball is rebuilt from the hits (as Twin Replay), not tracked.
