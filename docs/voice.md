# Spatial voice (My Park, Come Over, Watch Together)

Built 2026-10-06 (owner-approved "groundbreaking" item 3). Hear friends where they stand.

## How it works
- **Audio is P2P.** Each pair of people who can hear each other has one `RTCPeerConnection` (audio only, mono Opus ~32 kbps with FEC/DTX: `tuneOpus` + `maxBitrate`). Render carries only signaling: offer/answer/ICE/bye, a few KB per pair. Nothing is recorded or stored.
- **Server** `server/voice/relay.js`: a generic relay. `set(room, member, on)` keeps who has voice on per room (in memory; max 16 per room, 8 in Watch Together), `signal(room, from, to, msg)` relays only between two members with voice on in that room, cleans the message (`cleanSignal`: SDP ≤ 16 KB starting with `v=0`, candidate ≤ 1 KB, only known fields) and rate-limits (80 signals / 10 s per member). Rooms:
  - My Park: `p<instance>`, members are park numbers (`park:vc { on }` → `{ ok, on, ice }`, `park:sig { to, kind, data }`; events `park:vc`, `park:sig`). Leaving the park or the park closing turns voice off. ICE comes from `server/aim/ice.js` (STUN + optional TURN env vars), injectable as `createPark({ ice })`.
  - Come Over: the hangout id, members are screen-name keys (`hg:vc`, `hg:sig`); blocked pairs refused; leaving/ending/dropping the socket turns voice off.
  - Watch Together: the session id (`tg:vc { id, on }`, `tg:sig { id, to, kind, data }`).
- **Client** `client/src/utils/voice/`:
  - `spatial.js` (pure): `listenerRelative` (world → Web Audio listener space; forward = (sin yaw, cos yaw), right = (−cos yaw, sin yaw)), `distanceGain` (inverse falloff from 1.5 m, fade to silence 11 → 17 m), `pickPeers` (the nearest 6 within 25 m; a connected peer kept to 30 m and rank 8: no flapping), `courtGain` (court mode: others at 0.22), `cursorPan` (Come Over), `duckStep` (Watch Together: down in ~0.12 s, back in ~0.9 s), `talking` (RMS with hysteresis).
  - `mesh.js`: one connection per peer, "perfect negotiation" (larger id is polite), mic attached to the offer's audio transceiver for incoming calls, one ICE restart on failure, a connection you started closes 4 s after you stop wanting it.
  - `session.js`: `createVoiceSession({ space })` owns the mic (`echoCancellation/noiseSuppression/autoGainControl`, mono), `claimCallSession()` (iOS play-and-record), the graph per peer on the shared AudioContext (muted `<audio>` element feeding Chrome's WebRTC→WebAudio, analyser → gain → HRTF `PannerNode` (world; distance is our own gain, the panner only places direction) or `StereoPannerNode` (pan) → a bus on `masterOutput` (taskbar volume/mute)), a 100 ms tick (placement, peer choice, levels), mute/push-to-talk (track.enabled), `visibilitychange` (iOS stops the mic in the background → "paused", re-acquired on return). A `space` adapter per feature supplies `me`, `mode`, `join`, `send`, `listen`, `place`.
- **My Park** `park/useParkVoice.js` + `world.voicePlace()` (listener = your spot facing the camera's yaw, people by park number, court-mates during a room game, names) + `world.setVoiceTalk(nums)` (green ring on labels, `data-talk`). UI: the mic (`VoiceChip`, `.in-park`) is always on the park's screen under ☰: **🎙 Talk** (`data-park="mic"`) turns voice on from that tap; on, the chip mutes/unmutes (`voice-mute`), **×** turns it off (`voice-off`), **Hold to talk** with push to talk, and one line (`data-voice-note`, `utils/voice/status.js voiceStatus`) says what's going on (connecting, too far, couldn't connect + why, mic blocked). The park menu (☰) > **Voice** keeps the on/off, Push to talk and per-person Mute. During a park game the chip (only while on) sits top-center as before.
- **Come Over** `utils/hangout.js` (`toggleVoice`, `voiceSession`, `setSpatialVoice`, `useHangoutVoice`): the 🎙 on the taskbar strip (click: mute; right-click or menu: off), Spatial Sound toggle in the menu, talking ring on the dots.
- **Watch Together** `together/togetherStore.js` (`useTogetherVoice`, `duck`), `Together.jsx` (🎙 Voice / ✕ at the top, "X talking" line, the player's `setVolume` dips by `duck`; `player.js` gained `volume()/setVolume()`).

## 2026-10-09: "Mic feature did not work" (the owner, on iPhone)
**Production evidence** (scratchpad `voice/prodsig.mjs`, `voice/appvoice.mjs`, `voice/turnprobe.mjs`, `voice/stunudp.mjs`):
- Signaling on production is fine: two sockets to `nine8ish.onrender.com` joined park 6, `park:vc` acked, a `park:sig` arrived. But the ICE config it hands out is **STUN only** (`turn: false`): no TURN env var is set on Render.
- Two emulated iPhones (390x844, fake mic, Chrome) on https://98ish.vercel.app in the same park: connected `host->host`, audio both ways (received RMS 0.34). With phone B forced to relay-only (`iceTransportPolicy: "relay"`, what a cellular/CGNAT or strict network amounts to): **no connection, forever, and the screen said "Mic on · 0 near you"**, no error, while the mesh churned connections that never started.
- No public no-signup TURN works: `openrelay.metered.ca` (the old shared `openrelayproject` login) and `freestun.net` gave no relay candidates (UDP to them unanswered; Google's and Cloudflare's STUN answered from the same machine).

**Root causes:** (1) no TURN relay in production, so any pair where one phone is on cellular (carrier NAT) or a strict Wi-Fi can't connect; (2) that failure was silent; (3) voice was buried in the park menu; (4) iPhone specifics that could also make it "not work": the audio session was switched to play-and-record only after the mic prompt, the context could stay suspended/"interrupted" after the prompt, Web Audio plays quietly through the voice route while the mic is open, and auto-lock stops the mic.

**Fixes:**
- `server/aim/ice.js`: Cloudflare Realtime TURN (`CLOUDFLARE_TURN_KEY_ID` + `CLOUDFLARE_TURN_API_TOKEN`; credentials minted per hour, 6 h TTL, port-53 URLs dropped since browsers time out on them), Metered (as before), and your own coturn with `TURN_SECRET` (TURN REST API: time-limited HMAC credentials) or a fixed login. The startup log names the relay in use (`[ice] TURN relay on (Cloudflare)` / `no TURN relay set up`). Used by calls, My Park, Come Over and Watch Together alike.
- `mesh.js`: a connection gets 12 s, one ICE restart, then 25 s in all; then it's given up (`onFail(id, { turn })`), the other side is told (`bye`; a bye on a never-connected link older than 12 s counts as their failure too) and that friend isn't retried for 30 s (no churn).
- `session.js`: inside the tap, before the first await: the shared context, `unlockAudio`, `claimCallSession` (play-and-record), the iOS output element, `holdScreenOn()` (`utils/wakeLock.js`: a screen wake lock while voice is on); after the prompt the context is woken again, and each tick resumes it if iOS interrupted it. On iPhone/iPad (`isAppleMobile`) the spatial mix goes to a `MediaStreamDestination` played by one `<audio>` element (loudspeaker or headphones at full volume; it follows the taskbar volume through its own gain), resumed on coming back. State gained `problems` (who couldn't be reached, and whether a relay was offered), `far` (voice on but too far to hear) and `relay`.
- UI: the always-there mic in My Park (above), problem lines in Watch Together (`data-tg-voice-problem`) and a ⚠ on Come Over's mic (its tooltip says why). Help "Voice chat" gained "If you can't hear each other".

**After** (vite 5302 / server 8302, same two emulated iPhones): `host->host` audio both ways and the iPhone output element carrying the mix (RMS 0.21, playing); relay-only with no TURN: both phones show "Voice couldn't connect to GUEST-…: one of you is on a network that needs a relay (often cellular data). Try both on Wi-Fi. Trying again soon."; relay-only **with a TURN server** (a local `node-turn` on 3478 via `TURN_URLS`/`TURN_USERNAME`/`TURN_CREDENTIAL`): connected `relay->host`, audio both ways. Screenshots in the scratchpad `voice/shots/`.

**What the owner does (one step):** make a free Cloudflare account, then dash.cloudflare.com > **Realtime** > **TURN Server** > **Create** (name it 98ish), copy the **Turn Token ID** and **API Token** into Render's env for `srv-davi6cm7bikc73e5k5ig` as `CLOUDFLARE_TURN_KEY_ID` and `CLOUDFLARE_TURN_API_TOKEN` (Render redeploys). (Alternative: Metered's free 20 GB/month at https://dashboard.metered.ca/signup?tool=turnserver -> `METERED_TURN_APP` + `METERED_TURN_API_KEY`.) ## Limits / iPhone
- iPhone web apps stop the mic in the background (shown as "Voice paused"); headphones avoid echo (echo cancellation helps but a phone speaker next to the mic is hard).
- Cellular/strict NATs need a TURN relay (docs/env-vars.md: `CLOUDFLARE_TURN_*`, `METERED_TURN_*` or `TURN_URLS`); without one those pairs can't connect, and the mic's line says so.
- A full mesh: each person uploads one ~32 kbps stream per connected peer (6 max in the park ≈ 200 kbps up).
- Watch Together ducking needs the YouTube player's volume API (works on desktop; on iPhone, YouTube's embed ignores setVolume, so the video doesn't dip there).
